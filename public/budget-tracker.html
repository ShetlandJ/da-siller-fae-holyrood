Notes & definitions

  • Scope. GERS total expenditure is all public spending for Scotland — devolved and reserved — at £117.6bn in 2024-25. This is wider than the devolved Scottish Budget (~£68bn).
  • Control. The devolved / reserved / mixed marker is indicative. Social protection is mixed: UK-wide pensions and Universal Credit alongside devolved Scottish social security.
  • Real terms. Constant 2024-25 prices using the HMT GDP deflator. The cash view shows figures as published.
  • Other & accounting. Smaller functions plus national-accounts adjustments required to reconcile to the published total; not a spending programme.
  • Reconciliation. The twelve functions sum to the GERS published total in every year (residual < £3m, rounding).

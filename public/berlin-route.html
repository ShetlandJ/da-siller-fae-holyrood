Berlin-Mitte · one morning

The Hackesche Höfe round

Eight stops, all inside a 600 m pocket of Mitte, starting from Sant Buena at half seven. The walking is trivial — what really sets the order is when each place opens.

A true “Chinese postman” route is the shortest walk that covers every street — but this is a handful of destinations in one small quarter, so it's really a stop-order (travelling-salesman) problem. And with everything a few minutes' walk apart, distance barely matters: the route below is sequenced by opening time, coffee first, latest opener last. Total walking is about 20 minutes; the morning is paced by the shutters going up.
